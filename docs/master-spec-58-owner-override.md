# Master Spec 58 — Owner Override

Approved by the Owner on 2026-07-30.

The later Owner instruction supersedes the original AI video-editing requirement:

- 80 AI must analyze and select the original still-image or video post for advertising.
- Video must be previewable before selection and the original source must remain unchanged.
- The Owner's production team is responsible for video editing.
- 80 AI may use the selected source as an Existing Post or create a Dark Post, subject to the PAUSED-only safety rules.
- This override does not authorize campaign activation, spend, budget changes, or schedule changes.

Acceptance requires real Production evidence of analyzed source video, a playable
preview, and at least one source video selected into an ad without an AI-edited
revision.
